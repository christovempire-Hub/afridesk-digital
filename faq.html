Freelancing & International Payments FAQ

Quick answers to common questions about pricing freelance work, invoices, fees, exchange rates and receiving international payments in Africa.

How should I price freelance work?

Start with the income you need, estimate the real hours required, add direct project costs and account for platform or payment fees. Then compare the result with the value and scope of the project. Use the Freelancer Pricing & Profit Calculator to test the numbers.

What is my true freelance hourly rate?

Your true rate is what you actually earn after considering unpaid work, project expenses and payment costs. Divide your net earnings by the total time the project consumes, not only the hours spent producing the final deliverable.

Why can I receive less than the amount my client sent?

The difference can come from platform fees, payment processing charges, currency conversion costs, receiving or withdrawal fees and exchange-rate differences. The important number is your final take-home amount.

How do I calculate international payment take-home?

Start with the amount sent, subtract transaction and platform fees, account for conversion and receiving costs, and compare the final amount you receive with the original amount. AfriDesk's Currency & Fee Calculator can help estimate the result.

Should I charge clients for payment fees?

It depends on your agreement, market and payment method. The key is to understand the full cost before quoting. You can either price with those costs already included or clearly agree how payment-related costs are handled.

What should a professional freelance invoice contain?

At minimum, include your business or freelancer name, client details, invoice number, issue date, description of work, amount due, currency, payment instructions and agreed payment terms. Use the AfriDesk Invoice Generator to create one.

How can I keep better records of international payments?

Record the client, project, invoice, expected amount, currency, payment method, fees, exchange rate, withdrawal costs, final amount received and payment date. A consistent record makes it easier to understand your real income and compare payment methods.

How can I reduce the risk of freelance payment scams?

Verify payment inside the actual payment platform or bank account rather than trusting screenshots or emails. Be cautious with overpayment requests, urgent refund requests, unexpected fees and requests to move a transaction outside a trusted platform.

What is the best international payment method?

There is no single best method for every freelancer. Compare the total cost, exchange rate, receiving options, settlement speed, withdrawal practicality, client convenience and dispute or fraud protection before choosing.
