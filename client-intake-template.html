Client Intake Template

Collect the information you need before you price, propose or start a freelance project.

1. Client & Project Details

Use these questions to understand who you are working with and what they need.

Client / Business NameWho is the client?
Contact PersonName and role
Email / ContactPreferred communication channel
Project NameShort project title
What problem are you trying to solve?Ask the client to describe the business or customer problem in their own words.
What outcome do you want?Define what success should look like.

2. Scope & Deliverables

Clarify what is included before you calculate a price.

Requested deliverablesList each item, page, design, feature, document or service.
What is NOT included?Record exclusions to reduce scope creep.
Reference examplesLinks, files or inspiration supplied by client
Required tools / accessAccounts, brand assets, content or credentials needed

3. Timeline & Approval

Preferred start dateWhen should work begin?
Required completion dateWhen must the project be delivered?
MilestonesBreak larger projects into stages
Who approves the work?Identify the final decision-maker

4. Budget & Payment

Do not treat budget as the only pricing factor. Use it alongside scope, effort, costs and your minimum acceptable rate.

Client budget rangeOptional: ask for a realistic range
CurrencyNGN, USD, EUR, GBP or other
Payment methodPreferred payment route
Payment scheduleDeposit, milestones, final payment, etc.

5. Before You Send the Proposal

Confirm the problem and desired outcome.
Turn the requested work into clear deliverables.
Identify exclusions and assumptions.
Estimate hours, direct costs and payment fees.
Set your price and payment schedule.
