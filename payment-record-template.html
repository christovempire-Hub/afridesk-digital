Tip: Keep the invoice amount and the amount actually received separate. This makes fees and payment differences much easier to see.

How to use it

  1. Download the CSV template.
  2. Open it in your spreadsheet app.
  3. Replace the example row with your own client/payment information.
  4. Record transaction, conversion and receiving fees separately.
  5. Use the Net Received and Outstanding columns to understand the real result.
  6. Review the record regularly and keep supporting payment evidence.
AfriDesk principle: Don't judge a payment method only by its headline fee. Track the total cost and what you actually receive.
