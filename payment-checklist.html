International Freelance Payment Checklist

Use this checklist before receiving or withdrawing an international freelance payment. The goal is simple: understand what is being charged, what you will actually receive, and what you need to keep on record.

1. Confirm the amount and currency

Confirm the invoice amount, payment currency and the amount the client is expected to send.

2. Check platform or receiving fees

Identify percentage fees, fixed fees, minimum charges or other receiving costs that may apply.

3. Check payment processing costs

Look for card, transfer, withdrawal or payment-processing charges that may be deducted before the money reaches you.

4. Check the exchange rate

If you will convert currencies, compare the rate being used with a reasonable market reference. A good headline fee does not always mean a good final conversion.

5. Estimate your take-home amount

Calculate the amount you expect to receive after fees and currency conversion. Use the AfriDesk Currency & Fee Calculator when useful.

6. Check settlement and withdrawal practicality

Confirm how long settlement may take, how you can withdraw the funds, and whether your preferred withdrawal method is available to you.

7. Verify the payment and client

Be cautious with unexpected payment instructions, fake payment confirmations, pressure to refund money, or requests to pay a fee before receiving funds.

8. Keep a payment record

Record the date, client, invoice reference, amount, currency, fees, exchange rate used, amount received and any relevant transaction reference.

9. Review the real cost

After the payment, compare what you expected with what you actually received. This helps you choose better payment methods and price future work more accurately.

Important: Payment providers, platforms, banks and exchange services can change their fees, rates, limits and availability. Always verify the current terms before making a payment decision. This checklist is practical educational information, not financial, tax or legal advice.
