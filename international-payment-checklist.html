International Freelance Payment Checklist

Use this simple checklist before and after an international client payment so you know what you agreed to, what it costs and what you actually keep.

Before the client pays

Use these checks to avoid surprises and make payment terms clear.

☐

Confirm the payment currency

Know whether the client is paying in your preferred currency or whether conversion will happen.

☐

Confirm the payment method

Know how the client will send the money and whether you can practically receive or withdraw it.

☐

List every likely fee

Check transaction, platform, processing, conversion, withdrawal and receiving costs where applicable.

☐

Check the exchange rate

Do not judge a payment method only by its advertised fee. The conversion rate can affect the final amount.

☐

Agree on payment terms

Confirm amount, currency, due date, payment method and any deposit or milestone arrangement.

☐

Send a clear invoice

Include your details, client details, invoice number, services, total, currency, due date and payment instructions.

When payment arrives

Do not stop at the amount shown on the invoice. Record the real result.

☐

Verify the payment independently

Check your actual account or payment dashboard rather than relying only on a screenshot or message from a sender.

☐

Record the fees

Write down transaction, conversion, withdrawal or receiving charges that reduced the payment.

☐

Record the exchange rate

Keep the rate used and the original payment currency so you can understand the final result later.

☐

Calculate your real take-home

Client payment minus transaction costs, conversion costs and withdrawal or receiving costs gives an estimated amount available to you.

☐

Match payment to the invoice

Record the invoice or project reference so your business records stay organized.

☐

Review the payment method

Keep a record of methods that work well and compare actual results rather than relying on headline fees.
