Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AfriDesk Digital is designed to provide useful digital tools and educational resources while keeping data collection as simple as possible.

1. Information we collect

Most AfriDesk Digital tools run in your browser. Information you enter into calculators, generators, trackers and similar tools is generally processed locally in your browser and is not intentionally sent to an AfriDesk Digital server.

If you voluntarily subscribe to a resource, download, newsletter or other offer, we may receive information such as your name and email address through our email/marketing service provider.

2. How information is used

Information submitted voluntarily may be used to deliver requested resources, communicate with subscribers, improve our products and understand which content is useful. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

AfriDesk Digital may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, search, email delivery, payments, advertising or embedded content. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies and analytics

Some pages or third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. We may use analytics to understand website traffic, performance and content usage.

5. Financial information

Our calculators provide estimates based on the information you enter. AfriDesk Digital does not provide banking, investment or financial-advisory services through these tools. Do not enter passwords, payment-card details or other confidential credentials into general website forms.

6. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our services, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

You can choose not to submit optional information. If you receive marketing emails from us, you can unsubscribe using the option provided in those messages.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as AfriDesk Digital develops. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use the contact details provided on the AfriDesk Digital website.
